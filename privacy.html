Privacy policy

Personal Dashboard · last updated October 17, 2026

Personal Dashboard is a private, personal productivity tool. This page explains what it stores and what it does with your Google Calendar if you choose to connect it.

What the dashboard stores

Your dashboard content (cards, tasks, notes and settings) is kept in your browser and, when you sign in, in a private database and file storage on Cloudflare that only your account can read. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. The dashboard asks Google for only these permissions:

  • Calendars it creates (calendar.app.created): the dashboard makes one calendar named "Dashboard tasks" and adds, changes or removes events for your tasks there. It cannot read, change or delete any other calendar or event.
  • Free/busy times (calendar.freebusy): the start and end times of busy periods on your own calendars, to show how full your day is. It never receives event titles, descriptions, attendees or locations.
  • Your email address (openid email): to show which Google account is connected.

The Google access key is stored encrypted on the server and is never sent to your browser. Busy times are shown on screen and not stored. Nothing received from Google is shared with anyone or used for any other purpose.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect in the dashboard (account menu, or More on a phone) to revoke access and optionally delete the "Dashboard tasks" calendar. You can also remove access any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.
